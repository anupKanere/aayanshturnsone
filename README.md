# 🌸 Aayansh Turns One – Imperial Japanese Royal Digital Invitation

A luxury, imperial Japanese royal digital birthday invitation celebrating the first birthday of little prince **Aayansh**.

Crafted in an authentic, prestigious **Imperial Wine Lacquer & Champagne Gold** palette with deep Japanese artistic influence (featuring grand Mount Fuji with the Asahi rising sun, traditional Torii gate with hanging celebration lantern, cherry blossom canopies, origami cranes, Japanese seals, and Seigaiha waves).

---

## ✨ Features & Highlights

- **👑 Imperial Japanese Royal Aesthetic**:
  - Deep rich velvety Wine & Burgundy Lacquer (`#140508`, `#1E0A0F`, `#2A0E15`) paired with radiant Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) and pure Washi Silk Ivory (`#FAF5EC`).
  - Traditional Torii Vermilion (`#C83737`) and delicate Sakura Pink (`#EAA8B6`) accents.
  - Striking custom vector illustrations:
    - **Grand Mount Fuji** with radiant Asahi rising sun backdrop and iconic snow ridge streams.
    - **Grand Traditional Torii Gate** with black-lacquered Kasagi caps and swaying Chōchin paper lantern carrying the golden kanji *"寿"* (Longevity & Blessing).
    - **Cherry Blossom Branches (Sakura)** with multi-layered petals and gold stamen centers.
    - **Traditional Japanese Hanko / Inkan Seals** (*祝, 寿, 絆, 愛, 慶, 道*).
    - **Royal Crown** studded with vermilion and gold jewels.

- **📸 Cherished Family Photo Gallery**:
  - **Prince Aayansh Centerpiece**: Displayed in an ornate Japanese gold-bezel circular frame with cherry blossom accents.
  - **With Papa Santosh**: Luxury gold-leaf framed portrait celebrating the father-son bond.
  - **With Mamma Shravani**: Luxury gold-leaf framed portrait celebrating maternal love.

- **🎬 Memories in Motion (Video Reel Player)**:
  - Cinema-grade video player showcasing Aayansh's 1st year video reel (`AayanshReel.mp4`).
  - Features custom play/pause overlay, mute/unmute toggle, and fullscreen mode.
  - Uses `preload="metadata"` to prevent unnecessary heavy network downloads on mobile until the user chooses to watch.

- **🎵 Celebration Piano Music Experience**:
  - Plays the uploaded piano instrumental soundtrack (`birthday-piano.mp3`) with comfortable default volume and smooth fade-in/fade-out.
  - Dual-engine fallback: If audio file playback is blocked by browser policy, it seamlessly engages the embedded Web Audio API pentatonic bell synthesizer.
  - Floating music control button with animated frequency bars and glowing pulse effect.

- **📞 Clean Host RSVP & Call Action**:
  - Direct 1-tap call button via `tel:+917972556784` with host details for Santosh & Shravani.

- **🗺 Interactive Google Maps & Directions**:
  - Responsive embedded Google Map of the celebration venue.
  - Prominent `"GET DIRECTIONS"` button directly opening `https://maps.app.goo.gl/n5x1wxWX48BwHRSz9`.

- **⏱ Live Countdown Timer**:
  - Accurately targets **13 November 2026, 7:00 PM (IST)** in large champagne gold typography with Japanese kanji labels (*日, 時間, 分, 秒*).
  - Automatically transitions to `"THE CELEBRATION HAS BEGUN!"` when the time arrives.
